1. What Tallis is

Tallis helps you keep a record of your own spending. With your permission it reads the transaction alerts your bank and card providers send to your phone by SMS and writes them into a money book on your phone. You can also add entries yourself.

Tallis does not make payments, transfer money, connect to your bank account, lend, or give financial, tax or investment advice. Anything Tallis shows, including summaries, budgets, reminders and the pup's comments, is for your own information only. Decisions you make about your money are your own.

4. Accuracy

Tallis reads alerts automatically, and banks write them in many formats. An entry can occasionally be missed, duplicated, or recorded with the wrong amount, date, merchant or category. Entries Tallis is unsure about wait for your review, but please check anything important against your bank statement. Tallis is not an official record of your accounts.
