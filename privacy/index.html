Tallis privacy policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Tallis is a personal money book for Android. It is made by Vyoma Studios ("we"), India. You can reach us at dreamstrider07@gmail.com.

The short version
  • Your financial data stays on your phone. The version of Tallis you download from Google Play has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data. We have no servers that hold it and no way to see it.
  • Reading SMS is optional. You can use Tallis fully by typing entries yourself.

1. Bank SMS, if you turn on automatic tracking

Before Android asks for SMS permission, Tallis shows you this, word for word:

Tallis reads transactional alerts from your bank and card providers on this device so it can identify your financial accounts and record spending without manual entry.

What Tallis reads:

  • Messages from bank and card sender IDs, such as HDFCBK or ICICIB.
  • The amount, date, and merchant text inside those alerts.
  • Masked account or card details used to group alerts by financial source.

What Tallis does not read or share:

  • Messages from people. Alerts never arrive from a personal number, so those are discarded before anything is stored.
  • One-time passwords and verification codes.
  • Message content is never uploaded, sold, or used for advertising.

How long alert text is kept: Alert text is stored on this device only, and is deleted once it has been turned into a transaction or after seven days, whichever is first.

Your control: You can turn automation off at any time. Manual tracking stays complete either way. You can also remove the SMS permission in Android settings.

To understand an alert, Tallis uses rules and a small machine-learning model that is built into the app and runs only on your phone.

2. What Tallis keeps on your phone

Tallis stores the things you see in the app in an encrypted database on your phone:

  • accounts, transactions, categories, budgets and reminders you create or confirm;
  • settings and notification preferences.

The key that unlocks this database is held in your phone's secure key store. Tallis turns off Android's automatic cloud backup for its data, so the data is not copied to a backup service by the app.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
Receive SMS, Read SMSOnly if you turn on automatic tracking. Tallis records a payment when a bank or card alert arrives. When you open the app, it also checks recent alerts in your inbox, so payments your phone did not deliver to Tallis in the background are not missed.
NotificationsTo tell you when a payment has been written into your book, and to send reminders you set up.
Run at startupTo keep your reminders and automatic tracking working after the phone restarts.

4. Exports and backups you make

You can export your transactions as a file or create an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase you choose. These are only made when you ask, and they are saved where you choose. We never receive them. A backup cannot be opened without your passphrase, and we cannot recover a lost passphrase.

5. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete everything from Tallis erases your data from this phone.
  • Uninstalling Tallis also removes its data from the phone.
  • Exports and backups you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. More detail: Delete your data.

6. Children

Tallis is meant for adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

If what Tallis does with data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released, and the app will ask for your agreement again where the change affects what you agreed to.
