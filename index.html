Tallis

Every rupee, written down for you.

Tallis is a money book for Android. When your bank or card sends an SMS about a payment, Tallis writes it into your book: the amount, where you spent it, and the account it came from. You don't have to type anything, and there's no bank login.

Stays on your phone

The Play Store version has no internet permission. Your money data cannot leave your phone.

Reads only bank alerts

Messages from people are discarded. One-time passwords are ignored.

You stay in control

Turn SMS tracking off any time. Tallis also works fully as a manual book.
