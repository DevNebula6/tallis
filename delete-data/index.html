Delete your Tallis data

Tallis by Vyoma Studios

Tallis has no accounts and no servers. Everything it stores lives only on your phone, so that's where you delete it. We hold no copy.

Delete everything from the app

  1. Open Tallis.
  2. Go to Settings → Your data.
  3. Tap Delete everything from Tallis and confirm.

This erases your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, reminders, settings and any SMS alert text still waiting to be processed.

Or uninstall

Uninstalling Tallis removes all of its data from the phone. Tallis turns off Android's automatic cloud backup for its data, so no copy stays behind in a backup service.

Copies you made yourself

Transaction exports and encrypted backups are files you chose to save, for example in your phone's storage or a cloud drive. Delete those files wherever you saved them.

Stop SMS reading without deleting

In Tallis, open Settings → SMS tracking and turn it off, or remove the SMS permission in Android settings. Tallis keeps working as a manual book.
